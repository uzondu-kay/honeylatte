02/SERIES OVERVIEW

A different way
of seeing home

Architecture is the subject. The experience of home is the story.

OFFLINE follows distinctive residential projects across Africa, looking beyond the finished building to discover the people, ideas and cultural influences that shape it. Each episode explores one home through its architecture, its location and—most importantly—the everyday lives of its occupants.

01

Architectural
investigation

How do culture, design, technology, environment and personal lifestyles shape contemporary African homes?

02

Episode-level question

What particular challenge did this property address, and how does its design serve the people who live there?

03

A human-centred
perspective

The architect explains the intention. The homeowner reveals the reality: daily rituals, favourite spaces, family life, personal style and how the building has become their own.

THE EDITORIAL PROMISE

Not a property listing, a luxury ranking or a promotional tour: a thoughtful, visually ambitious film about architecture and belonging.

04/VISUAL TREATMENT

Cinema in the details

The visual approach is central to the storytelling.

01

The arrival

A recurring travel sequence brings the location to life through movement, music, fashion, streetscapes and an architectural reveal.

02

Architecture as
experience

Composed interiors, textures, light, landscape and observational footage show not only how a home looks, but how it feels to inhabit.

03

Drawings that come alive

Original plans, hand-drawn sketches and restrained motion graphics reveal the design process and translate cultural influences into visible form.

04

The imagined-home animation

Each episode includes a playful, imperfect line-drawn sketch of a participant’s ideal home, animated as their priorities and ideas unfold.

05

An understated presenter

A limited on-camera presence; narration and interviews sit over rich architectural imagery, natural sound and carefully curated music.

Honey & Latte Films is approaching selected architects and practices whose completed residential projects offer a distinctive architectural story and a meaningful relationship with their owners and surroundings.

01

Project access

Introduce the production team to the property owner and help explore suitable access for interior, exterior and landscape filming. All access remains subject to owner approval.

02

Design-team conversation

Participate in a filmed discussion about the original brief, design intent, material choices, influences and project challenges.

03

Supporting design material

Where available and licensed for use, share selected drawings, sketches, concept visuals or construction photographs for editorial illustration.

04

Homeowner participation

With their consent, facilitate a conversation about how the home is used and experienced. Privacy, sensitive spaces and boundaries will be respected.

05

Planning and recognition

Agree filming logistics, permissions, architectural attribution and any use of project materials in advance. Distribution and footage rights will be discussed separately.
